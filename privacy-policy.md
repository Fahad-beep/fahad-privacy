Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Domino, a Pakistan registered company (hereinafter referred to as  “we,” or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses personal information through its websites and mobile applications, including but not limited to the Oygula mental support mobile application and any other interactive services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Services”). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we handle your information.

By using our Services, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Scope of Application of the Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the Domino app and Services via desktop, mobile, or any other device. The Services are designed to provide mental health support and professional appointment-based communication across six user flows: parent, student, teacher, institute, doctor, and patient.

Information We Collect
Information You Provide Directly
We collect information you provide to us directly, including but not limited to:

Registration details (name, email, phone, user type)

Profile information including profile photo and certificate uploads (camera or gallery access is only requested for these purposes)

Appointment booking data

In-app messages and feedback

Encrypted payment details (via Apple and Google)

Automatically Collected Information
App usage data and analytics

Device identifiers and IP address

Time zone and language preferences

All information is encrypted at rest and in transit. Sensitive data is stored securely using encrypted cloud storage and device keychain mechanisms.

How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:

Enable core features like appointment booking and video calls

Authenticate users via access tokens that expire every 20 days

Facilitate secure, encrypted communication between users and professionals

Analyze aggregated data for app improvement

Send in-app or push notifications related to mental health appointments and service updates

We do not share or sell your information to third-party advertisers.

Aggregated or Anonymized Data
We may anonymize or aggregate data for research and app improvement. This data cannot be used to personally identify you.

How We Share Information
We only share personal information in the following cases:

With your consent

With third-party service providers who perform essential tasks (e.g., hosting, authentication, video calls) on our behalf

To comply with legal obligations

To protect user safety or prevent fraud

International Data Transfers
By using Domino, you understand your data may be transferred to and stored in the UAE where our servers and development team are located. All international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

Access to Your Information
You can update your information anytime via your profile settings. You may request deletion of your account or data by contacting us at info@mentalsupport.com. Deleted data is removed from live systems, except in legal cases or backups where retention is legally required.

Data Retention
We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined above. If your account is inactive or deleted, we delete or anonymize data within a reasonable timeframe, unless legally obligated to retain it longer.

Information Shared Socially
If you choose to share mental health progress or professional interaction summaries through our social features or external apps, we are not responsible for how third parties handle that shared information. Please use discretion.

Data Security
We use industry-standard encryption protocols and secure token authentication for all access. Apple Keychain and device-level encryption are used for storing credentials and sensitive metadata.

However, no digital transmission or storage method is 100% secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials. If you suspect unauthorized activity, contact support immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as our services evolve. We will notify you of material changes via app or email. Continued use of the app after an update implies acceptance of the revised policy.
